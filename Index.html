Introduction

Coastal alluvium soil occurs along the coastal plains of Southern Tamil Nadu. It is formed due to deposition of river and marine sediments and is suitable for paddy, coconut, and aquaculture.

Districts Covered

Tenkasi, Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Virudhunagar, Madurai, Sivagangai, Ramanathapuram
