Coastal Alluvium Soil – Southern Tamil Nadu

Districts

Tenkasi, Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Virudhunagar, Madurai, Sivagangai, Ramanathapuram (Ref: TNAU Agritech Portal)

Description

Coastal alluvium soil is a sandy to sandy-loam soil formed by deposition of river and marine sediments along coastal plains and estuaries. It has low water-holding capacity, high groundwater table and may be saline near the coast. (Ref: Soil Survey & Land Use Organisation – Govt. of India)
